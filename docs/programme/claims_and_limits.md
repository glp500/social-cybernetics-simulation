# Programme Claims and Limits

## Claims the programme may make

With frozen evidence, the programme may claim that:

- a named set of local rules is sufficient to generate a named distributional or ecological pattern
  in the model;
- the pattern is robust or fragile across a stated parameter, seed, and horizon domain;
- ecological opportunity, private sensing/adaptation, or social information contributes to a
  measured transformation in matched computational comparisons;
- a result is consistent with, complicates, or is illuminated by a named theory under stated
  assumptions;
- a missing mechanism limits the interpretation and motivates a separately specified extension.

## Claims the programme may not make

Without an empirical mapping and identification strategy, it may not claim that model outputs estimate
real-world magnitudes or identify a historical cause. Without the defining represented relations, it
may not translate:

| Model pattern | Prohibited automatic translation |
| --- | --- |
| inequality or persistent rank | class |
| resource competition | exploitation |
| cumulative harvest or energy | capital accumulation |
| information credits | money |
| accounted information transfer | market exchange |
| costly transfer | gift or reciprocity generally |
| deferred claim | anthropological debt generally |
| network centrality or source concentration | power |
| resource depletion or recovery deficit | capitalist metabolic rift |
| repeated rule-like pattern | institution or governance |

## Theory and evidence boundary

Interpretive literature cannot calibrate an unrelated parameter. Mechanism-support literature can
motivate an abstraction but does not prove its empirical generality. Counterevidence and boundary
conditions accompany support in the literature record. A theory's appearance in the matrix is not
implementation authorization.

## Welfare boundary

The programme reports outcome vectors rather than a composite welfare score. Survival, shortfall,
harvest, energy, inequality, ecological recovery, information quality, network structure, and
circulation burdens remain separately visible. Normative weights would constitute an additional model
and must not be hidden in analysis code.

## Model boundary

Agents are computational entities with abstract energy and explicit bounded rules. They are not
ethnographic persons, households, firms, classes, or populations. Resource cells are abstract
renewable stocks, not land, ecosystems, commodities, or means of production without an explicit
mapping.

## Extension rule

A new mechanism requires:

1. a scientific relation and protected exclusions;
2. paired mechanism evidence and counterevidence/boundaries;
3. an authoritative state/transition specification;
4. a discriminating control experiment;
5. immutable records and invariants;
6. a project-specific state component;
7. a user-approved implementation phase.

No future mechanism may be introduced merely to make an interpretation sound more realistic.
