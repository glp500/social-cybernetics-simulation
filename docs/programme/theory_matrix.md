needed for the permitted statement; “mechanism absent” prevents the prohibited inference.

## Calibration rule

No numeric shock, sensing, learning, diffusion, trust, cost, credit, or obligation parameter is
calibrated by the sources above. Exact values remain project design choices until a separate empirical
calibration study supplies a measurement model and data.
