# Programme Scope

## Included sequence

The active programme contains three independent studies:

1. ecological opportunity, material competition, viability, disturbance, and recovery;
2. bounded private sensing and private policy adaptation;
3. social reports, trust/network adaptation, sensing-profile diffusion, and minimal information
   circulation comparisons.

Studies share controlled ecological fixtures and evidence conventions, not a continuously expanding
agent class.

## Protected exclusions

The three-study programme does not implement:

- governance, institutions, norm enforcement, or rule-choice regimes;
- physical productivity-changing tools;
- material holdings, exchange, prices, firms, wage labour, ownership, or production classes;
- material debt, interest, collateral, refinancing, debt resale, inheritance, or bankruptcy;
- births, households, demographic reproduction, or intergenerational transmission;
- LLM-controlled agents, neural policies, or learned world models;
- composite welfare indices or automatic ranking of regimes;
- empirical calibration or claims that abstract units correspond to a field population.

These are not “hidden future fields.” A later programme extension must specify the new social relation,
justify it independently, compose new state, and pass a new decision gate.

## Project boundaries

### Project 1

Project 1 includes environmental heterogeneity, mobility, renewable extraction, energetic viability,
simultaneous competition, independent/correlated/system shocks, linear finite recovery, exact local
observation, copied belief, fixed policy, and allow-all gating. It introduces no learning, social
information, exchange, or institution.

### Project 2

Project 2 adds only the observation channel, private observation history, a simple belief, a fixed
information-sensitive policy, tabular Q-learning, and unequal sensing profiles. It contains no social
network, report, diffusion, exchange, or obligation.

### Project 3

Project 3 adds social reports, a realized network, trust, rewiring, sensing-profile diffusion, and
four bounded information-circulation regimes. Its credits are experiment tokens and its obligations
are claims on those tokens. It contains no material market, money, firm, wage, property, or demographic
mechanism.

## Units and external validity

Time, energy, resource, distance, cost, and credit units are abstract and dimensionless. Results are
conditional computational comparisons. External validity requires a separately specified empirical
mapping, calibration data, and validation study.

## Implementation authorization

Only Project 1 is authorized for executable completion in the current refactor. Project 2 and 3
documents are binding future specifications, not permission to add their fields or mechanisms now.
