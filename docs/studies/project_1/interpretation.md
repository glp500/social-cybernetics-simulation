## Standing interpretations

- A change in Gini with constant aggregate harvest is a distributional transformation, not an
  efficiency improvement or decline.
- Higher harvest with greater catastrophic shortfall is a tradeoff, not a superior regime.
- Persistent cumulative-harvest rank is a material persistence result inside the model. It is not
  wealth reproduction because agents own no productive assets and transfer nothing across generations.
- A cumulative recovery deficit captures foregone ecological state during recovery. It does not map
  the abstract resource to biodiversity or ecosystem function.
- Mortality is failure of the model's energetic viability condition, not demographic mortality risk
  in a real population.
