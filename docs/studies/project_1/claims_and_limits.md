# Project 1 Claims and Limits

## Permitted claims after freeze

Project 1 may identify:

- which ecological mechanisms are generatively sufficient for named unequal material outcomes;
- whether paired conditions change harvest, security, distribution, persistence, or recovery within
  the tested computational domain;
- whether differences persist over the 1,000-tick analytical horizon;
- which stage-level transitions and exposures account for a result;
- where aggregate and distributional/ecological outcomes trade off.

## Prohibited claims

Project 1 cannot establish:

- empirical magnitudes, forecasts, or causal effects for a real social-ecological system;
- biological fitness, household subsistence, or ethnographic livelihood without a mapping study;
- wealth, capital accumulation, class, exploitation, ownership, markets, or power;
- reciprocity, sharing, moral economy, institutions, governance, or collective action;
- capitalist metabolic rift or ecologically unequal exchange;
- informational advantage, learning, social diffusion, or network effects;
- demographic reproduction or intergenerational persistence.

## Important mechanism limits

- Observation is exact and local; movement under scarcity is uniform random.
- The fixed policy has no rest action, foresight, energy cap, or adaptation.
- Energy is an abstract viability stock; harvest converts one-for-one by default.
- Cells permit unlimited occupancy and movement collisions do not exist.
- Landscape fixtures are designed, not empirically estimated.
- Shock hazards, damage, propagation, and recovery are stylized controls.
- Recovery is linear and reaches baseline exactly absent repeated damage.
- There is no carrying capacity for agents, reproduction, or population replacement.
- Longer time does not add evolutionary or institutional change.

## Analytical limits

- Ten seeds support transparent replicated comparison but do not guarantee precise tail inference.
- Rank metrics can be undefined for small or tied cohorts; null values are evidence about design
  informativeness and are never silently converted to zero.
- The bottom-quartile burden uses cumulative harvest rank and must be interpreted as incidence by
  material outcome, not a pre-existing social group.
- Inequality half-life is a descriptive peak-decay statistic, not an estimated structural relaxation
  parameter.
- The combined recovery-deficit measure averages two normalized components and is not welfare.

## Completion claim

The phrase “Project 1 complete” is permitted only when all seven gates in
`validation.md` are frozen and linked to reproducible artifacts from the locked environment.
