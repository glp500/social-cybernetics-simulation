# Project 3 Claims and Limits

## Permitted after freeze

Project 3 may identify model-internal effects of fixed social reports, trust/rewiring, complete-profile
diffusion, sender cost, immediate credit conditionality, and deferred claims on named information,
relational, material, and resilience outcomes. It may identify source concentration, information
dependency, and gatekeeping potential under the exact represented access rules.

## Prohibited

Project 3 cannot claim:

- real-world trust, culture, gift, reciprocity, market, money, debt, class, exploitation, or power;
- material exchange, commodification, capital accumulation, profit, rent, or financialization;
- institutions, governance, enforcement, property, firms, wages, households, or demographics;
- general collective intelligence or social optimality;
- Q-learning counterfactual dependency from simple offline report removal;
- empirical network or anthropological calibration.

## Mechanism limits

- the initial ring lattice is a controlled topology, not an observed network;
- reports transmit one small environmental content type;
- trust is an accuracy weight with a short validation rule, not a general social relation;
- rewiring uses local proximity and a threshold, with no relationship cost or multiplex ties;
- profile diffusion is deterministic and all-or-nothing;
- credits have one bounded access function and fixed initial endowments;
- obligations have fixed principal/delay and only bilateral exclusion on default;
- no legal, moral, reputational, coercive, or third-party enforcement exists;
- network and circulation experiments remain within the frozen ecological and private-information
  controls.

## Implementation boundary

No Project 3 state, configuration, or dependency enters executable code until Projects 1 and 2 are
frozen and the user authorizes Project 3 implementation. Credit and claim types must be composed only
for the circulation variants that use them.
