# Project 3 Experiments

**Status:** planned; execution requires frozen Projects 1 and 2 plus Project 3 mechanism gates.

All experiment groups use the three frozen ecological regimes, canonical private sensing profiles,
500 ticks, and ten paired seeds unless a later preregistered precision analysis changes the design.

## P3-A — Social information

Compare private sensing only with fixed-network social reporting: 2 conditions × 3 ecologies × 10
seeds = 60 runs. Trust weights remain `0.5`; topology and profiles do not change.

## P3-B — Adaptive trust

Compare fixed network/weights with directional trust plus rewiring: 60 runs. Both conditions receive
the same reports before the treatment-specific trust/topology update. Report accuracy, concentration,
fragmentation, and material outcomes remain separate.

## P3-C — Capability diffusion

Start from the same balanced randomized restricted/enhanced profiles. Compare no diffusion, simple
diffusion, and reinforced multi-source diffusion: 90 runs. Initial assignments are reused across
conditions within ecology/seed blocks.

## P3-D — Circulation

Compare D0 unconditional, D1 costly, D2 immediate accounted, and D3 deferred obligation: 120 runs.
The same sender cost applies in D1–D3. Initial credit endowments, network, reports, source selection,
trust, and ecological states are matched within seed blocks.

## Total and cap

The planned canonical Project 3 design contains 330 runs. The generator refuses a larger expansion
without a versioned specification. D3 is executed only after A–C pass their freeze checkpoints.

## Evidence

Every social-information event records sender/receiver, timestamps/content, profiles, trust,
circulation regime, sender cost, credit transfer, and obligation ID. Obligations record creation, due,
settlement, principal, status, and default. Aggregate analysis retains raw report, credit, claim,
network, information, material, and ecological outcomes for every run.
