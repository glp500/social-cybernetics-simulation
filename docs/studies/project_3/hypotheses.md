# Project 3 Hypotheses

## P3-A — Social information

- Fixed-network reporting changes information coverage and opportunity gaps relative to private
  sensing only.
- Material/security effects can be positive, null, or adverse depending on ecological regime and
  correlated/stale reports.
- Network position becomes associated with information and material outcomes only in the social
  condition.

## P3-B — Adaptive trust

- Trust adaptation improves mean report accuracy relative to fixed weights when source reliability is
  heterogeneous.
- Rewiring can increase source concentration or fragment access even when mean accuracy improves.
- No monotonic resilience benefit is assumed.

## P3-C — Capability diffusion

- Simple diffusion spreads enhanced profiles faster than reinforced diffusion.
- Reinforcement can preserve clusters and persistent information inequality while reducing exposure to
  isolated sources.
- Diffusion can equalize information coverage without equalizing material outcomes.

## P3-D — Controlled circulation

- D1 isolates the material burden of costly contribution relative to D0.
- D2 isolates immediate access conditionality/accounting relative to D1 while preserving sender cost.
- D3 isolates temporal claims and credit-access dependency relative to D2.
- Accounted regimes can concentrate sources and obligations; direction and welfare tradeoffs are not
  preregistered.
- Total information credits remain exactly conserved in D2/D3 under every outcome, including default.

## Cross-cutting resilience

After matched ecological shocks, compare recovery of survival, unmet need, information coverage, and
network connectivity. Faster recovery in one dimension does not establish overall resilience.
