# Project 2 Theory

## Information as a transformation

Information is not an independent welfare stock. Its value is conditional on an environment, an
observation channel, and a decision rule. Project 2 therefore traces:

```text
information advantage -> decision advantage -> material advantage -> survival advantage
```

Each arrow is measured separately; failure of conversion is a result.

## Partial observability

POMDP research motivates treating observation as distinct from environmental state. Project 2 uses a
minimal framed observation rather than claiming a full POMDP solution. Radius, noise, and delay isolate
different limitations and keep their randomness independent.

## Private adaptation

Tabular Q-learning supplies a transparent, bounded adaptive comparator with a convergence foundation
under assumptions not guaranteed by this nonstationary multi-agent ecology. The fixed-policy control
is therefore primary. Learning results are finite-horizon behavioural outcomes, not evidence of
optimality or human cognition.

## Information inequality

Unequal sensing profiles can produce unequal access to actionable opportunity even from identical
initial material conditions. Distributional analysis must distinguish profile assignment, observation
quality, decision quality, material conversion, and persistence.

## Skill and situated action boundary

Anthropological theories of perception and skill can illuminate how action depends on practical
engagement, but the project's numeric sensing profile and Q-table do not reproduce embodied skill,
lifeways, apprenticeship, culture, or phenomenological experience.

## Claims discipline

Use informational, adaptive, and material advantage. Do not use capital accumulation, human capital,
epistemic class, or capability deprivation without separately represented and justified relations.
