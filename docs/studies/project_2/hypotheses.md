# Project 2 Hypotheses

## P2-A — Homogeneous information factorial

Across Project 1's frozen representative ecological regimes:

- larger radius can increase opportunity coverage but may not improve decisions under delay/noise;
- noise and delay reduce observation accuracy through distinct mechanisms;
- private Q-learning can improve or degrade finite-horizon security relative to the fixed policy;
- channel components can interact, so no monotonic benefit is assumed;
- mean information gains can coexist with unequal material conversion.

The full comparison is `3 radius × 2 noise × 2 delay × 2 policy`. The primary estimands are main and
interaction contrasts in information quality, decision quality, material outcomes, and survival.

## P2-B — Informational inequality

Compare all-restricted, all-enhanced, and balanced mixed populations under matched ecology, positions,
seeds, and initial material state.

- **Primary hypothesis:** mixed private sensing access produces persistent material differences by
  profile even without social interaction.
- **Secondary hypothesis:** private Q-learning can reduce or amplify profile-based conversion gaps.
- **Null:** randomized profile assignment is unrelated to cumulative harvest, unmet need, survival,
  and material-rank persistence beyond seed variation.

## Required conversion chain

For each run, report profile association with:

- information coverage and observation error/age;
- decision quality;
- cumulative harvest and unmet need;
- survival;
- material-rank persistence.

Profile correlation must not be described as a causal effect unless estimated from the randomized
mixed assignment under the preregistered matched design.
