## Standing boundaries

- Better observation accuracy is an information advantage only; it becomes material advantage only
  through measured decision and outcome changes.
- Profile-based outcome differences in the randomized mixed treatment identify the effect of this
  model's sensing assignment, not real educational, technological, or class inequality.
- Q-learning is a private adaptive mechanism, not intelligence, rationality, skill, or cognition in
  general.
- Persistent profile differences are not capital accumulation because there is no ownership,
  transfer, investment, or production relation.
- A null average can hide unequal conversion; both mean and distribution remain primary.

## Mandatory alternative readings

At minimum, interpretations compare value-of-information, bounded-rational/adaptive, human behavioural
ecology, and situated-skill readings, then state where the implemented abstraction fails each theory.
