# Project 2 Claims and Limits

## Permitted after freeze

Project 2 may claim that its specified sensing channel and private policy change named information,
decision, material, security, or persistence outcomes within frozen ecological regimes. Randomized
mixed-profile assignment may support a model-internal causal claim about profile assignment.

## Prohibited

Project 2 cannot claim:

- human perceptual or learning calibration;
- intelligence, education, expertise, human capital, or technology in general;
- social learning, communication, culture, institutions, or collective intelligence;
- class, exploitation, capital accumulation, power, or structural social exclusion;
- empirical effects of real informational inequality;
- Q-learning convergence or optimality without satisfying and testing its assumptions.

## Mechanism limits

- observations use a small geometric frame with stylized Gaussian error and fixed delay;
- belief is a direct copy rather than inference under a world model;
- sensing profiles are complete fixed endowments except in the later Project 3 diffusion study;
- Q-learning has coarse bins, a small action set, fixed hyperparameters, and private experience only;
- ecological conditions come from Project 1 and contain no social provisioning institutions;
- the mixed experiment randomizes profile labels, not real social access processes.

## Implementation boundary

No Project 2 type or configuration is executable until Project 1 is frozen and the user authorizes the
next implementation phase. Future code must compose Project 2 state and preserve the Project 1
control path exactly.
