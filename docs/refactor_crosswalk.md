## Existing evidence retained

The pre-refactor v0.1/v0.2 work is not discarded. It supplies Project 1's verified mechanical kernel:

- pure resource, cognition, intent, gate, resolution, physiology, and shock functions;
- the explicit recovery → regeneration → shock → observation → belief → intent → gate → physical
  resolution → metabolism → mortality → measurement schedule;
- complete cohort and ecological spatial history;
- normalized shock-event, exposure, and damage evidence;
- deterministic RNG substreams;
- fail-closed run and batch bundles;
- V0–V4, sensitivity-design, CLI, and browser verification.

Those results remain historical evidence. They do not by themselves satisfy Project 1's new analysis,
experiment, or interpretation freeze.

## Intentional clarifications

- “Fully complete Project 1” means all seven gates are frozen, including executed experiments and
  interpretation—not merely that the mechanics run.
- “The rest of the projects specs and plans” means complete scientific and implementation documents
  for Projects 2 and 3. It does not authorize their mechanisms in the Project 1 release.
- The programme uses abstract, dimensionless units until an empirical calibration study exists.
- All exact grids, thresholds, seeds, horizons, and replicate counts are recorded as versioned project
  design choices unless primary evidence directly calibrates them.
